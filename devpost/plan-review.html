Tasks — three kinds

Checklist

Submission todos from the plan. Due dates anchored to the contest. Human ticks them.

Scheduled

Cloudflare Cron. Morning Card regen · rules re-check. Shows next/last run.

Agent-promoted

Chat work that will take a while becomes a task. Caddie returns immediately. Status + retry + Card blip.
